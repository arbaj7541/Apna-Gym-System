About Our Gym

Welcome to My GYM Management System.

This project is developed using Python, Django, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and SQLite3. It helps gym owners manage members, trainers, attendance, workout plans, payments, equipment, and customer enquiries from a single dashboard.

Features

  • Member Management
  • Trainer Management
  • Attendance Tracking
  • Payment Management
  • Workout Plans
  • Equipment Management

Members

Manage all gym members efficiently.

Trainers

Assign and manage gym trainers easily.

Management

Track payments, attendance and workout plans.
